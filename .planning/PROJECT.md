# Jamaica House Brand

## Vision
Ecommerce website for Jamaica House Brand — Chef Anthony's artisan hot sauce brand. Full online store with Stripe checkout, product pages, and brand storytelling.

## Stack
- Next.js (React)
- Stripe (checkout, webhooks)
- Vercel deployment
- Google Analytics 4 + Meta Pixel

## What's Built
- Complete ecommerce site (6 phases, ~11 plans)
- Core commerce pages
- Cart and Stripe checkout
- Content and brand storytelling
- SEO and performance optimization
- Production launch and go-live verification

## Key Details
- Live at: jamaicahousebrand.com
- Domain: Bluehost, DNS delegated to Vercel (ns1/ns2.vercel-dns.com)
- Stripe live keys configured
- GA4 + Meta Pixel tracking active

## Status
COMPLETE — shipped and live. All 6 phases finished, checkout verified end-to-end.
